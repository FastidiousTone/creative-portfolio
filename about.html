A little about me.

Curious by nature. Intentional by design.

I’m Alex Rivers, an independent designer and creative developer. I help people turn ambitious ideas into thoughtful brands and digital experiences.

Thoughtful work starts with curiosity.

I believe the strongest design begins with listening. By understanding the people, purpose, and challenges behind a project, I create work that feels distinctive, useful, and true to its context.

How I work

  1. Discover

    Listen, ask questions, and understand the people and purpose behind the project.

  2. Define

    Turn those insights into a clear direction, shared priorities, and a focused brief.

  3. Create

    Explore ideas, develop the strongest direction, and refine the details together.

  4. Deliver

    Prepare the final assets and provide the guidance needed to put them into use.

Client
Form & Field
Role
Brand identity
Year

The challenge

Form & Field needed a visual identity that reflected its thoughtful approach to everyday products. The challenge was to balance a natural, approachable feel with a distinctive presence across packaging and digital touchpoints.

The approach

The identity pairs confident typography with a soft, nature-inspired palette. A flexible layout system brings consistency to packaging and digital applications while leaving room for playful details.

The outcome

The resulting identity gives Form & Field a consistent visual language across packaging and digital touchpoints. Its flexible typography, botanical details, and distinctive palette create a cohesive foundation for future products.
